INCOME TAX SEP 2026

AY 2026–27 Returns & Recent CBDT Updates

ITR-1 to ITR-7 for AY 2026–27 are available through the Income Tax e-Filing portal. Recent CBDT updates in September 2026 include notifications relating to TDS reporting for certain non-resident property transactions and other prescribed forms.

Official Income Tax Updates →
GST JAN 2026+

GSTR-3B Interest Calculation Enhanced

From the January 2026 tax period, the GSTR-3B interest calculation has been enhanced to consider the minimum cash balance available in the Electronic Cash Ledger during the relevant period. GSTN has also introduced related auto-population enhancements.

Read Official GSTN Advisory →
AUDIT 2026

Revised 2026 Tax Audit Guidance Note

ICAI has released the Revised 2026 Guidance Note on Tax Audit under Section 44AB of the Income-tax Act, 1961. The publication provides updated practical guidance for tax-audit engagements and reporting.

View ICAI Guidance Note →
ACCOUNTING 2026

Financial Statements of Non-Corporate Entities

ICAI has announced phased applicability of its Guidance Note on Financial Statements of Non-Corporate Entities. Phase II applies to all entities for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2026.
